## Caustics

Fast, differentiable strong gravitational lensing simulations in Python.
Built on JAX for GPU acceleration and automatic differentiation.

## caskade

A framework for building modular, composable scientific simulators in Python.
Designed to reduce boilerplate and make research pipelines easier to refactor.
