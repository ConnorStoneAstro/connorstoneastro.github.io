I'm a researcher in astrophysics at the University of Toronto, working at the intersection of
astrostatistics, data science, and open-source scientific software.

My research focuses on developing statistical and computational tools for astronomical imaging —
including point spread function modelling, gravitational lensing, and photometry pipelines.
I'm driven by the belief that good software and rigorous statistics are inseparable from good science.

When I'm not writing code or analysing data, I enjoy astrophotography and science communication.
