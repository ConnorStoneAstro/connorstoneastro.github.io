I am a [Canadian Rubin Fellow](https://casca.ca/?p=19612) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), working on cosmology and supernova science with [Rubin](https://rubinobservatory.org/) survey data. My work applies a fully Bayesian framework to tackle the selection function and the coupled challenges of photometric redshift estimation, classification, and distance measurement.

Before Toronto, I held a postdoctoral position at [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca/en/), supported by [NSERC](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/pd-np/pdf-bp_eng.asp) and [CITA](https://www.cita.utoronto.ca/opportunities/national-fellows-programs/cita-national-fellows/) fellowships, where I worked on astrostatistics, astronomical image processing, and gravitational lensing. My doctoral work at [Queen's University](https://www.queensu.ca/) focused on developing Bayesian methods for measuring diversity in galaxy populations.

Beyond research, I enjoy [astrophotography](/astrophotography/), [science outreach](/outreach/), hiking, tabletop gaming, and travel. My latest major project is raising my sons Orion and Leo, with my wife Jessica.

## Research interests

- Cosmology and supernova science with Rubin/LSST
- Astrostatistics and Bayesian inference
- Astronomical image processing
- Strong gravitational lensing
