My work sits at the boundary of astrophysics, statistics, and software engineering.
I build methods and tools that make it easier to extract reliable science from astronomical images.

## Current interests

- **Point spread function modelling** — pixellated Bayesian inference for PSF characterisation in
  weak lensing, astrometry, and photometry contexts
- **Gravitational lensing** — fast simulation and inference for strong lensing systems
- **Scientific software** — designing modular, composable Python frameworks for research pipelines
