My work sits at the intersection of cosmology, statistics, and software. I build methods and tools that make it easier to extract reliable science from large astronomical surveys and imaging data.

## Current: Rubin/LSST supernova cosmology

<!-- SECTION IMAGE — replace src with your own -->
<img class="section-img" src="/images/moneyplot.png" alt="Supernova cosmology results">

As a Rubin Fellow at the University of Toronto, I am developing a fully Bayesian framework for supernova cosmology with the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST). The key challenge is jointly addressing the survey selection function alongside covariant photometric redshift estimation, supernova classification, and distance measurement — problems that are deeply entangled and must be handled consistently to avoid biased cosmological constraints.

## Gravitational lensing

<!-- SECTION IMAGE — replace src with your own -->
<img class="section-img" src="/images/demos/photoz_specz.png" alt="Gravitational lensing">

I develop fast, differentiable simulation tools for strong gravitational lensing, enabling large-scale inference on lens populations. This work is implemented in the open-source [Caustics](https://github.com/Ciela-Institute/caustics) package.

## Astronomical image processing

<!-- SECTION IMAGE — replace src with your own -->
<img class="section-img" src="/images/m51.jpg" alt="Galaxy M51 — photometric fitting">

I build modular, GPU-accelerated tools for fitting complex astronomical images — handling crowded fields, multi-band data, and simultaneous PSF characterisation. This work is implemented in [AstroPhot](https://github.com/ConnorStoneAstro/AstroPhot).

## Astrostatistics and Bayesian methods

<!-- SECTION IMAGE — replace src with your own -->
<img class="section-img" src="/images/demos/SBIdemo.png" alt="Simulation-based inference diagram">

My doctoral and postdoctoral work at Queen's University and Université de Montréal focused on Bayesian methods for galaxy population analysis — characterising diversity in morphology and stellar populations using hierarchical models and simulation-based inference.
