Public Outreach

Science for everyone — podcasts, public talks, school programmes, and live events
1 min · Connor Stone

Scientific knowledge is worthless if it can’t be shared with everyone.

Queen’s Observatory#

During my PhD at Queen’s University I served as coordinator of the Queen’s Observatory, running a range of public outreach programmes.

Fast Radio Bursts Podcast — A series exploring current astronomy topics through researcher interviews, making cutting-edge science accessible to general audiences.

Open House Talks — Recorded presentations posted to YouTube so anyone could access them regardless of location or schedule.

Percy Goes to Mars — A livestream event for the Perseverance rover landing that drew over 700 viewers.

Science Rendezvous 2022 — Organised 15 volunteers running 9 hands-on activities at this annual public science festival. Received the Outstanding Volunteer recognition.

Virtual School Tours — Group tours for classes and summer camps across grade levels, adapted for remote delivery.

Public Speaking and Media#
