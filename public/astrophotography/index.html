Astrophotography

Auroras, eclipses, and deep-sky objects photographed from Ontario and Quebec
1 min · Connor Stone

Bad photos that I am proud of. I served as coordinator of the Queen’s Observatory during my PhD, which sparked a serious amateur astrophotography habit.

Aurora Borealis — May 2024#

Captured from my backyard following a major solar storm. One of those nights where you run outside in your socks.

Total Solar Eclipse — April 8, 2024#

Photographed with a DSLR and kit lens. No amount of preparation makes totality feel real.

Messier Objects Tour — 2020#

A project to photograph Messier catalogue objects. Highlights include M61, which happened to host a supernova during my imaging run.

M51 — The Whirlpool Galaxy#

Multiple versions, raw and processed. The processing pipeline for this one grew into custom stacking code to align images with larger pixel offsets than commercial software could handle.

M57 — The Ring Nebula#

My first serious stacking attempt. I keep coming back to this one.

M27 — The Dumbbell Nebula#

The first time I got a photo with colour and interesting structure. It stuck.
